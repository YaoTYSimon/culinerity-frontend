# Culinerity: what has been built

Status as of 28 October 2026. "Branch" says where the work lives; nothing here has been pushed beyond `origin/main` (6db289d).

## On `main` (pushed)
- **Food-insecurity check-in:** FIES-based questionnaire giving a 0 to 100 "cognitive load of hunger" score (100 = secure, 0 = critical).
- **Craving swaps:** pick a craving, get cheaper and healthier alternatives ranked by your score (cheaper at low scores, healthier at high).
- **Melbourne prices:** mocked Coles/Woolworths/market price source behind an interface, ready for a live source.
- **Food support:** local help and relief options list.
- **Monorepo:** web app, `@culinerity/core` (platform-free logic) and `@culinerity/server` (Hono + SQLite reference data), 13 CC0 food photos, tests.

## `feature/plate-redesign` (complete, unpushed)
- **Plate design system:** the plate is the dial; round photos, pill buttons, Schibsted Grotesk, no cards or shadows, AA contrast enforced by a script.
- **Welcome page** for new users with real home-cooked-meal photos.
- **Four cover themes** (tobacco, sand, walnut, cream) with a picker; single token source with a drift test.
- **One-question-at-a-time check-in**, the number screen, swaps with "Add to my week", food support, friendly empty/error states.
- **My week** card with savings and a share text (no personal data in it).

## `feature/sign-in` (complete, unpushed)
- **Local, on-device profiles:** create, sign in, switch, lock, delete (typed-name confirmation); avatars and colours.
- **Optional passcode:** PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA-256 (210,000 rounds, random salt), session in `sessionStorage`, soft 30 s pause after 5 wrong tries.
- **Move guest data** into a new profile; per-profile storage; corrupt-storage and cross-tab protections.
- Honest copy: a local lock, not encryption or account security. README/AGENTS document limits and how to reset.
- Visible 2px field edges (WCAG 1.4.11) across every input; plate favicon.

## `feature/budget-policy` (complete, unpushed)
- **Sourced benchmark dataset** (20 sources, 37 indicators, 12 Melbourne areas, 6 interventions) with a generator (`npm run build:benchmarks`), freshness test, "Older data" labels and verification labels. Verified against ABS, Foodbank, VicHealth and other sources. No invented numbers.
- **Budget tracker:** Monday-week maths (DST-safe), plate showing what is left, pace message, add/remove/undo spending, past weeks, saved-with-swaps, income sentence and "How this compares" with cited figures.
- **Data and policy dashboard:** plain-language sourced figures with dials, bars and scales, Melbourne areas by disadvantage, what helps, gaps listed, sources, and a print layout. Reached from the Menu and the footer.

## `feature/visuals` (complete, unpushed)
- **Photo headers** for budget, policy and sign-in, and **empty-state visuals**, in the plate design.

- **Typical vs stated prices.** A price is "stated" only when the source says so ("free", a price); "affordable" alone is not, so the seven Community Grocer markets are a typical budget estimate, listed under All and Fresh produce but not Low cost. A market or grocer at the middle band is a "typical" estimate for that kind of place and is labelled that way; the Free filter trusts stated prices only.
- **Privacy rule: location is never stored or sent.** A device position (only after "Use my location" is pressed) is rounded to 3 decimals, held in memory for the view, and discarded on leaving, on "Clear my location" and on unmount. Typed suburbs and postcodes are matched against a bundled table of ABS centroids with no geocoding service. Nothing is written to storage, the profile, the share text or the network.
- **Licence decision.** The City of Casey "Emergency Food Relief Groups" list is not imported: the state portal says CC BY 3, but the publisher's own page states no licence ("All rights reserved"), so it was treated as ambiguous. OpenStreetMap is not used (ODbL would apply to our dataset). Places are entered as facts, one at a time, from operator pages. Sources and licences show in a "Where this comes from" disclosure.
- **The "Not checked by us" tier (shipping: Overture release 2026-11-18.0, 19,418 places: 19,404 after the quality rules below plus 14 food relief rows).** Under "Checked by us" (the 177 places above, always first), Nearby can show a second list of Greater Melbourne supermarkets and grocers, fresh produce, butchers, fishmongers, delis, bakeries, takeaway and cafes or restaurants from Overture Maps (CDLA-Permissive-2.0 or CC0), nearest first. **What it is:** a list of places as the source gives them: name, category label, street address, postcode when the source has one, a Maps link, "Price not known" and "Check hours before you go". Below it: "Listed by Overture Maps, release X. An older release shows "This list may be out of date." after 6 months and is replaced by "The wider list is being refreshed." after 12; a failed cell shows "Some places could not load. Try again later."; nothing listed close by says "No places in this list close to here." "Report a problem" is a prefilled mailto that appears only once `REPORT_PROBLEM_CONTACT` (`src/content/reportProblem.ts`) is set; it carries the place id and name, and the person types the problem type (closed, moved, wrong, other) themselves. **Privacy, stated plainly:** the request for places carries a coarse cell key only. A position from the device and a typed suburb are treated alike: a typed suburb maps to its fixed centroid and so always to the same 3x3 block of cells, which means the host can infer the area to about 1.5 km (the same as for a device position, and no more). No typed suburb or coordinate appears in any URL. **Pipeline quality rules (21 November 2026, `scripts/lib/placeQuality.mjs` and `scripts/lib/overtureFilter.mjs`).** After the Overture filters, kept places go through mechanical rules; none edits or invents a value, a record passes unchanged or is dropped, and chains are never a reason to drop. (1) Truncated names: a name ending in and, &, of, the, at, for, in, with, to, a, an or by (except complete endings such as "eat in"), a dangling separator, or an unclosed bracket; 6 dropped ("Wyndham Community And", "Hire A", "Bean To", "Tia To", and "Dip in" and "Wok Rite In", which may be real names: the price of the rule). (2) Junk names: fewer than 2 characters, no letters, a URL or domain, a phone number (8 or more digits), padded spam text, or only kind words ("Pizza Shop", "Cafe Store"); 20 dropped. All-caps is NOT a rule: ALDI, KFC and MAD MEX are real, and ILTS cannot be told from ALDI. (3) Addresses: a street word and a number, or two streets at a corner, or a named venue (shopping centre, plaza, market, station); 386 dropped (bare street names such as "High Street", "451", "Melbourne"). This is the rule most likely to drop a real place, including chain stores whose source address is only a street name. (4) Shared point: more than 8 distinct names at one 5-decimal point is a centre point, not a shop; 18 dropped (two points, Fountain Gate and Chadstone food courts). (5) De-duplication, replacing the old 50 m same-name scan: names are compared ignoring case, punctuation, accents, "and" and "&", and a leading "the"; two records are duplicates when the names have the same words (within 50 m, or at the same street address within 1 km) or one name is the other plus 1 to 3 trailing words such as a suburb ("KFC" and "KFC Tarneit North": within 100 m, or at the same address within 1 km). The same address means the same street number and street (abbreviations expanded, unit text ignored) and the same postcode when both have one. When the kinds differ, an exact name needs 50 m or the same address, and a variant needs the same address; the kept record has the most specific kind (a retail kind over takeaway over cafe-restaurant), then the highest confidence, then the lowest id. This resolves limitation #8: a bakery and a cafe of one name at one address collapse into the bakery, and it uses a grid by cell so it is not O(n squared) (the run takes under a second). Deterministic. ## `feature/cultural-food` (complete, unpushed)
- **My food** (spec `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-27-cultural-food-design.md`): optional, skippable setup of food traditions (10 with catalogue items, plus the person's own words), diet rules, things to avoid and occasions, all stored on the device in the profile. Clearing is one tap with Undo.
- **Cultural food in Swaps:** 49 sourced cultural foods and 57 swaps with nutrition from AFCD Release 3 (CC BY 4.0). Cultural items appear only for a person who chose at least one of their traditions (and the item is tagged with it); the original items stay for everyone. A person with no chosen tradition (no food profile, an empty one, or only rules or allergies) sees exactly the pre-branch swaps, pinned by a golden file recorded from the code at 88d044a, and cultural cravings are not found by search for them. Settings then hide items with a chosen allergy, hide or mark "Check the ingredients" for rules (unknown never passes), lead the picklist with the person's traditions, and mark "Fits your kitchen". The "Nutrition estimated from public food tables." note shows only when a cultural item is on screen.
- **Meals that felt like home:** a Monday-week ring and a two-tap log with optional dish and who with; this week and last; Remove, Undo, Clear. No streaks or targets.

## Build size
- **View-level code splitting.** `src/App.tsx` loads Budget, What if, Trade-offs, My pantry, Cook, Policy and Nearby with `React.lazy`, behind one `Suspense` with a plain-text "Loading…" fallback (`src/ui/LoadingText.tsx`). They are same-origin static chunks: no new network hosts. The main chunk dropped from about 889 kB to about 415 kB, so Vite's 500 kB warning is gone. When a view loads late, focus still moves to its heading (`useFocusOnViewChange`). Keep the first screen's imports free of the recipes dataset (it once came in through `cookNotes`); tests that open a lazy view use `await findBy…`.
- **Load failure.** If a view's chunk fails to load (offline after a deploy), `LazyErrorBoundary` (`src/ui/`) wraps the `Suspense` and shows plain text, "This part did not load. Check your connection and try again.", as a focused `role="alert"` with a "Try again" button. The lazy imports use `lazyWithRetry`, so Try again calls the import again instead of reusing React's cached failed promise. The boundary clears when the view changes. Nothing about the error is logged or sent.
- **Data checks.** Food-safety sources must come from the four hosts actually cited (betterhealth, health, safefood and foodstandards), and a leading minus counts as part of a figure (-15). A test requires a dollar figure (or "low cost") in the note or source title of every stated-budget food-relief or community-meal place.
